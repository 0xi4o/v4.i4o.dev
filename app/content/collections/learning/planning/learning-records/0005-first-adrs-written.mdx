# First ADRs written; consequences overcorrected to negatives only

Ilango wrote ADRs 0001 (store docs as Markdown with a SQLite index) and 0002
(derive a manifest from `go.mod`) within the fifteen-minute box each, added
both to the ADR index in `career/notes.org`, scored 6/7 on the design doc /
ADR / neither drill and 3/4 on the quiz. Titles are decisions, not topics;
each Decision names what it was chosen over; contexts are value-neutral.

## Evidence

- Consequences in both ADRs list only what got harder — the mirror image of
  the lesson-3 problem (reasons without costs). "All consequences" was read
  as "the negative ones".
- 0001 never states which store is the source of truth, and treats the
  `modernc.org/sqlite` driver as decided without a decision (a candidate
  ADR 0003).
- 0001's context contradicts the design doc's non-goal (mentions a TUI).
- A quoted line from the lesson was left pasted in 0002's Consequences.
- Bullets are fragments rather than sentences; acceptable at this length.

## Implications

- The ADR format is learned. The recurring weakness across lessons 3 and 4
  is symmetry: he writes one side of a trade-off at a time. Keep a
  "both columns" check in later self-review lists.
- Lesson 5 (POC write-up) can assume ADRs and the design doc as known ground
  and spend its time on evidence and recommendation.
- Suggest ADR 0003 for the SQLite driver as a five-minute follow-up, not a
  lesson.
