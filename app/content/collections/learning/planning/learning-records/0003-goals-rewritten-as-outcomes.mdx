# Goals rewritten as outcomes; non-goals carry a why

After lesson 2 Ilango rewrote the DocuDex goals section: five of six bullets
are now outcomes that more than one design could satisfy (humans and agents;
no network for browsing; versioned docs per `go.mod` dependency; three
commands with no manual; whole team, no server, no accounts). Each non-goal
now states why it is out. He reported making mistakes in the goal vs design
choice sorting drill and none on non-goals, which matches the doc: the
distinction is learned but not yet automatic.

## Evidence

- Residual: "via godoc" survived inside a goal — the exact error from the
  drill, in the doc.
- Two goals not yet checkable ("usable by both humans and agents"; "usable
  with no internet connection", which is untrue for `add`/`sync`).
- Open questions collected at the end (three), but without a line on what
  would resolve each. Context section did not get the "why now" line.
- Design section untouched, as intended: reasons without costs remain.

## Implications

- Lesson 3 targets the design section: choice → over what → because → at the
  price of → open question, on the CLI, storage and serving paragraphs, with
  the alternatives section mirroring the design choices rather than the scope.
- Keep interleaving the goal/design-choice test in later quizzes until it
  stops slipping.
- Testability of goals is a small recurring nudge, not a lesson.
