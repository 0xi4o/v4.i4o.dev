Building a three-node homelab on ThinkCentre M710q Tinys and a TL-SG2008
switch: Proxmox on the metal, Kubernetes in VMs, Terraform driving the VMs,
Tailscale and Cloudflare Tunnel for access. Every lesson ends with a change
on the real rack and a line in the runbook.
