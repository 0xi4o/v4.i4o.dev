# Mission: PostHog

## Why

Ilango is building products (Kobun, Ink, the site) and flying blind on how anyone
uses them. He wants general fluency in PostHog's web and product analytics — enough
that the numbers tell him what to build next, on any product he ships.

The goal is understanding PostHog, not instrumenting one app. Kobun serves as the
running example because it has both surfaces a real product has — a marketing site
and an app behind a login — so the full visitor-to-user journey can be taught on it.
Every lesson stays generic; swap Kobun for any other product and nothing changes.

Starting point: absolute beginner at analytics as a discipline. Comfortable with
code, not with the vocabulary. Nothing is assumed.

## Success looks like

- Design the event list for a product from scratch — and defend why each event
  earns its place — before writing any tracking code.
- Instrument a marketing site and the app behind it, in one project, so a single
  person's path from first visit to in-app action reads as one connected story.
- Answer "where are people dropping off?" with a funnel he built, and read the
  result without being fooled by it.
- Answer "which traffic actually sticks?" — separate a spike in visitors from a
  gain in people who come back.
- Look at any PostHog chart and say what it is counting, in plain terms.

## Constraints

- PostHog Cloud free tier. No self-hosting.
- Short lessons, one win each. Plain English, no unexplained jargon, British
  spellings.
- Solo founder time budget: side-project hours, not a working week.

## Out of scope

- Self-hosting, scaling, ClickHouse internals.
- Building an analytics tool. He is a user of PostHog here, not a re-implementer.
- Session replay, feature flags, experiments, surveys, CDP — later, once web and
  product analytics are solid.
- SQL / HogQL querying until the visual insights are second nature.
