# Mission: SaaS marketing for founders

## Why

Ilango is launching Kobun — an open-source, Git-based CMS — into early access.
The core product works and a landing page exists, but nothing is public yet and
there are zero users. He wants to learn marketing well enough to take Kobun from
zero to a base of real, active users, and to reuse the skill for every future
Aurelius Labs product.

## Success looks like

- Can name who Kobun is for in one sentence, and say where those people spend time online.
- Gets the first 10 early-access users through manual, direct effort — and can explain why manual comes first.
- Has analytics running that answer "who signed up, and did they reach the moment Kobun became useful to them?"
- Can run a simple weekly loop: do one marketing activity, measure it, keep or kill it.
- Has published content and knows how to pick topics people actually search for (keyword research).
- Can explain where each marketing channel fits (organic vs paid) and when Kobun should — and shouldn't — use it.

## Constraints

- 2–3 hours per week for marketing practice. Lessons and tasks must fit that.
- Complete beginner at marketing; strong engineer. No jargon without translation.
- Kobun's repo is private for now — nothing public-facing without checking first.
- Solo founder with a day job: prefer simple, reversible, low-cost moves. Paid channels are theory until there's money and data to justify them.
- Lessons are written in Ilango's voice rules (see `About Me/writing-rules.md`): plain, direct, British spelling.

## Out of scope

- Paid ads execution (theory only, until organic is working and there's budget).
- Enterprise sales, procurement, security reviews — wrong stage.
- Brand design / visual identity — already defined elsewhere (`personal-brand.md`).
- Marketing for ilango.builds.stuff — that project has its own strategy docs.
