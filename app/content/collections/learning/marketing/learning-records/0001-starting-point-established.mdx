# Starting point established

Ilango is a complete beginner at marketing with no prior vocabulary — funnel,
activation, conversion all needed translating. He is a strong full-stack
engineer, so technical implementation (analytics tooling, SEO mechanics) will
not be the hard part; the conceptual ordering of marketing work is. Kobun has a
working core and a landing page, nothing public, zero users, 2–3 hours/week
available.

## Implications

- Teach concepts and ordering; skim implementation mechanics.
- Zone of proximal development: positioning and manual user acquisition next.
  Analytics, SEO, and content are motivated but premature — sequence them after
  the first users exist (see roadmap in NOTES.md).
- Lesson 1 set two anchors to build on: the five-stage funnel and "manual
  outreach first". Retrieval-practise both in lesson 2 before extending.
