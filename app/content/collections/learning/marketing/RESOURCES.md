# SaaS marketing resources

High-trust sources for this course. Lessons draw from here, not from guesses.

## Knowledge

- [Essay: "Do Things That Don't Scale" — Paul Graham](https://paulgraham.com/ds.html)
  The founding argument for manual, unscalable user acquisition at the start.
  Use for: why the first users are recruited by hand, not by "launching".
- [Article: "How today's fastest growing B2B businesses found their first ten customers" — Lenny Rachitsky](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-todays-fastest-growing-b2b-businesses)
  Data from 20+ companies (Figma, Notion, Airtable…). Use for: the concentric-circles-of-trust model for finding the first 10 users.
- [Guide: "Dev tool marketing for early-stage startups" — PostHog founders' hub](https://posthog.com/founders/dev-marketing-for-startups)
  PostHog is the closest analogue to Kobun: open-source devtool, COSS model.
  Use for: what works and what's a waste when marketing to developers.
- [Article: "How we got our first 1,000 users" — PostHog](https://posthog.com/founders/first-1000-users)
  Concrete channel-by-channel account (Hacker News, word of mouth, content).
  Use for: realistic expectations per channel for an OSS devtool.
- [Article: "How to get the first 10 paying customers for your devtool company" — PostHog](https://posthog.com/blog/first-10-paying-customers)
  Use for: moving from free users to paying ones, devtool-specific.
- [Article: "Writing for developers" — PostHog](https://posthog.com/founders/writing-for-developers)
  Use for: content marketing tone and topics for a developer audience.
- [Book: _Obviously Awesome_ — April Dunford](https://www.aprildunford.com/books)
  The standard text on positioning. Use for: deciding what Kobun is _for whom_, before any channel work.
- [Guide: "SEO Basics: Beginner's Guide to SEO" — Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-basics/)
  Use for: SEO fundamentals and keyword research, when we get there (lesson series later in the course).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com)
  Founders at exactly this stage sharing what worked. Use for: feedback on launch/marketing plans, seeing real numbers from comparable products.
- [Hacker News — Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/show)
  Where OSS devtools traditionally get their first spike. Use for: launching Kobun when it goes public; study other Show HN posts first.
- [r/SaaS](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaaS/)
  Mixed signal but active. Use for: quick questions, seeing common beginner mistakes.
