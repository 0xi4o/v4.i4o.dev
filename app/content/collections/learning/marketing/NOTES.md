## Course roadmap (drafted session 1 — revise as records accumulate)

1. ✅ The map: how SaaS growth actually works, and why "where do I start" has a known answer (funnel + do-things-that-don't-scale).
2. Positioning: who Kobun is for, in one sentence (April Dunford's method, applied).
3. Finding the first 10 users: concentric circles, watering holes, direct outreach that isn't spam.
4. Analytics I: what to track (signup → activation), and setting it up on Kobun.
5. Landing page conversion: making the page earn signups.
6. Content marketing I: picking topics developers search for (keyword research basics).
7. Content marketing II: writing for developers; publishing cadence that fits 2–3 h/week.
8. SEO fundamentals for a zero-authority domain.
9. Launches: Show HN / Product Hunt / early-access announcement, done properly.
10. The weekly loop: measure, keep, kill. Paid channels: when (and whether) ever.
