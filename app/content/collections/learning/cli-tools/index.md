Learning the command-line tools systems engineers reach for when something is
slow, stuck or unreachable: ripgrep and fd, jq and yq, ps/pgrep/lsof, dtrace
and bpftrace, du/tar/lsblk, and the networking set (curl, ss, dig, nc, tcpdump,
openssl). The aim is to look instead of guess.
